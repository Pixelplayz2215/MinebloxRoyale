MINEBLOX ROYALE v1.0.1.1
======================

What you need
  - Windows PC, about 2 GB free disk space and 8 GB RAM or more.
  - Your own Minecraft: Java Edition account (the Microsoft account you play Minecraft with).
  - Optional: Roblox installed (players get the Roblox avatar look, outfits, emotes and sounds; the
    roblox.com version is checked to work, the Microsoft Store version and Bloxstrap-style launchers are
    looked for too) and
    Fortnite installed (a splash poster in the hub). Without them the game still works:
    players keep the normal Minecraft look. Mineblox Royale only READS a few files from
    those games; it never changes, starts or connects to them.

First time
  1. Install it with your launcher: open PICK YOUR LAUNCHER.txt (next to the launcher folders), go into your
     launcher's folder and follow its HOW TO INSTALL.txt (Prism Launcher, CurseForge, Modrinth App, Lunar Client,
     ATLauncher, GDLauncher, MultiMC, or Mojang's Minecraft Launcher).
  2. Start it: the game opens straight into your own Mineblox Royale world.
  3. Your world's first island takes about 2 minutes to make (the hub banner counts down; try the
     obby or the shooting range meanwhile). After that, each next island is made during the match.
     Tip: keep the game on an SSD rather than a USB or external hard drive; islands are made much faster.

Playing together
  - Everyone starts in their own world (their own private room).
  - The host types /rc in chat: it shows an 8-digit room code (also on the hub banner).
  - Friends type /rc 12345678 (the host's code) and are taken into the host's room.
  - /leave takes you back to your own world.
  - A room holds up to 16 players.

Modes and parties (Solo, Duos, Trios, Squads)
  - The hub has four portals: Solo, Duos, Trios and Squads. The match starts in the mode everyone
    is standing on (everyone in the hub has to stand on the same portal).
  - Team up with a party: /party invite <name> asks a player in your room; they type
    /party accept. /party leave leaves it, /party shows who's in it. Up to 4 players; anyone in
    the party can invite. Leaving the room (/rc to another room, /leave) leaves the party.
  - Your party is your team in Duos, Trios and Squads (a party bigger than the mode is split into
    teams). Empty places in a team are filled with bot teammates, and the bots play in teams too.
  - Teammates each have their own colour: their name over their head, in chat (on your screen),
    their dot on the map and minimap, and their waypoint (map, beam in the world, compass bar).
    Their health and shield are in the top-left corner. No friendly fire.
  - Knocked, not out: at 0 health with a teammate still standing you're knocked. Crawl to cover;
    a teammate holds F next to you for 5 seconds to revive you. You bleed out in about 30 seconds
    (faster each time). If your whole team is down, you're out.
  - Reboot: when you're eliminated your teammates can pick up your reboot card (it goes in their
    offhand, even with a full inventory) and hold F at a reboot van for 10 seconds to bring you
    back. A card lasts 90 seconds. While you wait you watch a teammate (sneak to look around).
  - Last team standing wins: everyone in it gets the Victory Royale.

Commands (also on the COMMANDS board in the hub)
  /rc or /roomcode        show this room's code
  /rc <code>              join a friend's room
  /leave                  back to your own world
  /party invite <name>    ask someone in this room to team up
  /party accept           join the party you were invited to
  /party leave            leave your party
  /party                  see who's in your party
  Those are the only commands: no cheats or creative, in your own world or a friend's.
  - Everyone who plays together must be on the same version (this one is v1.0.1.1).

Your unlocks and outfit
  - Emotes and gliders unlock with eliminations (150 at most), Victory Royales (10 at most), the obby, and the
    shooting range (on its own deck off the hub's west side, by the obby). A range round lasts 60 seconds
    from your first hit: a hit scores 1 and a
    bullseye 2. When time's up, step off the range and back on for another round. Some items need a round score (your best counts), others a number of bullseyes in total.
    In the Locker (K) a locked item shows only what unlocks it; hover over it to see how close you are.
  - The six Dominus sets unlock together, each with its own challenge: hat, back bling, shirt, pants,
    shoes, preset and glider. Every other Locker item is free.
  - Emotes and gliders you unlock, and your Locker outfit, belong to your Minecraft account: they come with
    you into every world (a friend's too) and into every new version. They're kept on your PC in
    %APPDATA%\MinebloxRoyale, so deleting the game folder doesn't lose them.
  - Unlocks from earlier versions are picked up automatically the first time this version starts.

Updating to a new version
  - Easiest: import the new version's file the same way as the first time. It's a new profile with a new world
    (new room code); your unlocks and outfit come along.
  - To keep your world too (room code, leaderboard): open your existing Mineblox Royale instance's folder,
    and in "mods" replace the old minebloxroyale-....jar with the new one.

Controls (also on the board in the hub)
  K = Locker (outfits)   B = Emotes   G = Build mode   R = Reload   H = Back to hub when eliminated
  Tab = Map   Left mouse = shoot / pickaxe   Right mouse = aim
  F (hold) = search a chest / revive a knocked teammate / reboot teammates at a reboot van
  Sniper scope too fast or slow? Options > Controls > Mouse Settings > Scoped Sensitivity (50% at first).

Loot and materials
  - Chests glitter and chime. Walk up to one and hold F to search it: the lid opens and the loot pops
    out onto the ground. Gold chests hold normal loot; the rare blue chests hold the best guns
    (legendary and mythic) and often a Chug Jug.
  - Break things with the pickaxe for materials: trees for wood, stone and bricks for brick, and for
    metal the cars in parking lots (every hit gives metal; six hits wreck a car), shipping containers,
    iron and copper. Reboot vans and the Battle Bus can't be broken.

Something wrong?
  Send the game's log to the creator: logs\latest.log in your Mineblox Royale profile's folder (most launchers
  have "Open folder" on the profile; with the ready-to-play Prism it's
  Prism Launcher\instances\Mineblox Royale\minecraft\logs\latest.log).
